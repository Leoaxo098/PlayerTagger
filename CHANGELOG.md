# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Replaced PlayerTagger's forced glow with a depth-tested outline for visible parts of tagged players. Opaque blocks occlude the effect, including its white rim. Invisible players are excluded.
- Added a white rim for contrast. The assigned color fills more of the visible silhouette at longer distances.
- Preserved existing tags, assigned colors, commands, and the enabled setting. Retired the through-wall and invisible-player options.
- Vanilla name tags and the vanilla Glowing effect follow their normal behavior.
